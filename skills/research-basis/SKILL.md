**Rules**

- Do **not** invent citations. Only names below appear in `FULL-PIECE.md`, live prose, or `cos-effectiveness-research.md`.
- Label each claim: **Cited in piece** (live FULL-PIECE / locked prose) vs **Cited in research warehouse** (cos-effectiveness-research / intentions drafts; may lag the live piece) vs **Lived claim** (the user observed leadership gains; not a paper).
- Prefer one-liner callouts matching skill tips in the piece. Keep user-facing answers short; this file is the warehouse.

## Cluster: Goals (Finding Your North Star)

> **Callout - Set Goals:** locks a layered goal stack so task management has a clear north star, not a floating to-do list. Quarter → Month (#1) → Week (#1/#2/#3) → Day (#1). Action sentences with done-states; booking a call is a task on Open Items, not a goal.

| Practice | Skill(s) | Research | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specific, challenging goals + feedback beat "do your best" | [Set Goals](sand-workflow:set-goals) | Locke and Latham (2019) | **Cited in piece** |
| Proximal goals attached to distal goals on complex work; distal alone can underperform do-your-best | Set Goals cascade | Latham and Seijts (1999) | **Cited in piece** |
| Too many weakly related goals freezes progress; focus on a small subset (clear week #1) | Set Goals; Friday next-week lock | Ethiraj and Levinthal (2009) | **Cited in piece** |
| Specificity bar + goals-vs-tasks bar | Set Goals; Friday phase 3; setup Goals step | - | **Lived / locked practice** (not a paper) |

## Cluster: Daily intentions / Making Headway

> **Callout - Daily focus brief:** morning cue with suggested P0 (strategic w.r.t. week goals; same-day sized). User agrees or overrides → same-day commitment recorded with the bot. Open Items shield one-offs so focal work is not stolen.

> **Callout - End of day:** Did today's P0 finish? Anything truly tonight? Do not re-plan tomorrow at wrap; morning brief owns tomorrow's #1.

| Practice | Skill(s) | Research | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monitor progress; effect stronger when recorded and reported to someone else | Daily focus; EoD; Friday product update | Harkin, Webb, Chang, Prestwich, Conner, Kellar, Benn and Sheeran (2016) | **Cited in piece** |
| Hard goals move performance only with commitment | Morning agree/override; Friday week lock | Klein, Wesson, Hollenbeck and Alge (1999) | **Cited in piece** |
| Focal-goal commitment shields against competing demands | Daily P0 + Open Items dump/batch | Shah, Friedman and Kruglanski (2002) | **Cited in piece** |
| Mornings better for concrete planning than evenings | Daily focus at 6am; no evening re-plan | Peetz, Sullivan, Davydenko and Grossmann (2025) | **Cited in piece** |
| If-then (implementation intention): cue → response, stronger when rehearsed; beats mere scheduling | Daily brief (=cue) → P0 commit (=response) → EoD done/not; Friday forms week intention | Sheeran, Listrom and Gollwitzer (2024); Gollwitzer and Sheeran (2006) | **Cited in research warehouse** / intentions drafts. Live Making Headway currently leads with Harkin/Klein/Shah/Peetz; still use if-then as the CoS *design* language for the morning loop. |
